Cooking is only permitted in designated kitchen spaces. Cooking appliances should
not be used in living areas or bedrooms to prevent fire hazards.
Quiet hours: 10pm to 7am.
Security: Keep doors locked and do not let strangers into the residence. Report any
security concerns to the management immediately.
No smoking or drugs. This is strictly prohibited on the premises. Illegal drugs or
activities, including possession, use, or sale, are strictly prohibited. Violation will result
in disciplinary action, potentially involving law enforcement.
For safety reasons, certain appliances such as heaters with exposed elements, small
fan heaters, bar heaters, and refrigerators are not allowed. Only heaters or fans
provided by SCAPE are permitted.
Dispose of waste properly: Avoid leaving waste in common areas or near garbage
bins. Keep access to facilities clear. SCAPE Management reserves the right to remove
obstructions and levy charges for damages.
Fire alarm: Read the Scape fire safety regulations displayed in this apartment. If a fire
alarm is triggered by your actions, you will be charged a fire services call out fee of
$1600 or higher.
House rules
These guidelines are intended to enhance your safety, comfort, and enjoyment at Scape, as
well as that of all the other residents.
Common Areas: Keep shared areas clean and tidy. Clean up after yourself in the
kitchen and common spaces.
If any kitchen utensils/item is missing or broken, please advise ELC Accommodation
team by email: accommodation@elc.edu.au
Maintain open and respectful communication with roommates and staff to address
any concerns or issues promptly.
Compliance with rules: Failure to comply with house rules may result in
disciplinary action, including eviction from the residence with no refund.
Guest Policy: Please inform roommates in advance if you plan to have guests and
ensure they abide by house rules.
Permitted Hours: Weekdays until 9pm and Weekends until 10pm. No overnight
stays allowed.
